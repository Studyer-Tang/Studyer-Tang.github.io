The original is real filmed coastal surf, not a generated scene or live camera.
The source does not identify a filming location; no location is inferred here.

Changes: trimmed to 18.2 seconds; the edited loop starts at source 0.6 seconds,
then crossfades its final 0.6 seconds into source 0–0.6 seconds. The resulting
17.6-second recording plays forward in a loop. Audio is removed. Re-encoded
as H.264 high-profile/yuv420p MP4 with faststart at 25 fps, in 1920x1080 and
608x1080 (central portrait crop, without upsampling). The poster is the first frame of the edited recording, encoded WebP.
No synthetic waves, generative images, reverse playback or AI interpolation.
